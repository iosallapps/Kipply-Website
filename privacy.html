Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 23, 2026

Effective Date: August 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Kipply ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how Kipply handles your data when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Our Privacy Promise: Your photos never leave your device. Every scan, comparison and deletion happens locally, and we never upload, store or transmit your photos or videos. Kipply is free to use and supported by advertising, so the app does include advertising and analytics services from Google and a subscription service from RevenueCat. What those services receive, and how you can turn them off, is set out below.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Kipply does NOT collect, access, store, or transmit:

  • Your Photos or Videos: We never upload, store, or transmit your photos or videos to our servers or any third-party servers. The app has no photo upload capability at all.
  • Personal Information: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information. Kipply has no account and no sign-in.
  • Location Data: We never request location permission. Where a photo already carries a location recorded by your camera, it is read on your device only to show it to you, and it is not sent anywhere.
  • Contacts or Other Device Data: We do not access your contacts, messages, calendar, or any other data on your device besides photos and videos.

2A. Information Collected by the Services We Use

Kipply itself runs no servers. It does include third-party services, and those services collect data of their own:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): receives your device's advertising identifier and technical information about the device and the ad, in order to serve and measure ads. If you do not allow tracking, ads are served without a personalized profile.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (usage measurement): receives anonymous events describing how the app is used, for example that a scan finished or a screen was opened. It never receives photo content.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting): receives crash reports so we can fix what breaks.
  • Apple SKAdNetwork: tells an advertising network, without identifying you, that an install came from an ad.
  • RevenueCat (subscriptions): receives an anonymous identifier and your App Store purchase status, so the app knows whether Kipply Pro is active. Your payment details are handled by Apple and never reach us or RevenueCat.

3. Photo Library Access

3.1 Why We Need Access

Kipply requires access to your photo library to provide its core functionality: helping you organize and delete unwanted photos. This access is mandatory for the app to function.

3.2 How We Use Photo Access

  • Local Processing Only: All photo analysis, thumbnail generation, and organization happen entirely on your iPhone/iPad. Your photos never leave your device.
  • Deleted tab Feature: When you swipe left to delete a photo, it is queued in the Deleted tab temporarily. This queue is stored locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults.
  • Permanent Deletion: When you permanently delete photos from Deleted tab, they are removed from your device's photo library using Apple's Photos framework. We do not create backups or copies.
  • Statistics Tracking: Basic statistics (e.g., number of photos reviewed, deleted) are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to us.

3.3 Your Control

You can revoke photo library access at any time through iOS Settings → Kipply → Photos. However, this will prevent the app from functioning.

4. Data Storage

All data generated by Kipply is stored locally on your device:

  • Deleted tab Queue: Temporarily stored in iOS UserDefaults on your device
  • Statistics: Stored in iOS UserDefaults on your device
  • Preferences: App settings stored in iOS UserDefaults
  • iCloud Sync: If you have iCloud Photo Library enabled, photo deletions will sync across your devices via Apple's iCloud, not our servers

We do NOT have access to any of this data. It never leaves your device except through Apple's own iCloud sync (if enabled).

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Advertising (Google AdMob)

Kipply is free and is supported by ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may receive:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks
  • IP address
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • Device information (model, OS version)

On your first run iOS asks whether Kipply may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, or if you never answer, no advertising identifier is used and ads are served without a personalized profile. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform is shown before any ad is requested, and your choices there are respected. You can reopen it later from Settings inside the app, under Ad Privacy Settings.

Google's own privacy policy applies to the data it receives: policies.google.com/privacy.

5.2 Analytics and Crash Reporting (Firebase)

Kipply uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used in aggregate, for example how many scans complete or which screens are opened, and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports. These services receive anonymous, app-generated events and technical device information. They never receive your photos, your videos, or anything that identifies you by name.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, this measurement runs only if you consent to it in the form described above.

5.3 Subscriptions (RevenueCat and Apple)

Kipply Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store. Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier and your subscription status so that the app knows whether Pro is active. Their privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

5.4 What none of them ever receive

Your photos and videos never leave your device. No third-party service in Kipply has access to them, and ads are never selected based on their content.

6. Children's Privacy

Kipply is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at iosallapps@icloud.com, and we will take steps to delete such information.

7. Data Security

Since Kipply does not collect or transmit your data, there is no risk of data breaches on our end. Your photos and data remain securely on your device, protected by iOS's built-in security features:

  • iOS Sandboxing (apps cannot access each other's data)
  • Device encryption
  • Apple's secure photo library permissions

We recommend keeping your device locked with a passcode and enabling two-factor authentication for your Apple ID.

8. Your Rights

Since we do not collect your personal information, traditional data rights (access, deletion, portability) do not apply. However, you have full control over your data:

  • Delete App Data: Uninstall the app to remove all local data (Deleted tab queue, statistics, preferences).
  • Reset Statistics: Use the "Reset All Statistics" option in Settings to clear tracked data.
  • Revoke Access: Deny photo library access in iOS Settings to prevent the app from accessing your photos.

9. International Users

Kipply is designed for global use. Since all data processing happens locally on your device, there are no cross-border data transfers to worry about. Your data never leaves your device.

GDPR Compliance (EU Users): We comply with GDPR because we do not process personal data. Your photos remain on your device.

CCPA Compliance (California Users): We do not sell your personal information because we do not collect it.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, especially if we integrate third-party services like AdMob. When we make changes:

  • We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • We will notify you via an in-app message or App Store update notes
  • The updated policy will be posted at https://kipplyapp.com/privacy.html

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Email: iosallapps@icloud.com
Website: https://kipplyapp.com
Support Page: https://kipplyapp.com/support.html

12. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Your photos are processed entirely on your device and never reach our servers. We operate no servers of our own, so the personal data covered by GDPR is what the third-party services described in section 5 receive.

  • Consent is the basis for advertising and analytics in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, collected through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can withdraw it at any time from Settings inside the app, under Ad Privacy Settings.
  • Contract is the basis for processing your subscription status, which is what tells the app whether Kipply Pro is active.
  • Your rights of access, correction, deletion, objection and portability are exercised against the service that holds the data. Google's controls are at myadcenter.google.com, and you can write to us at the address below and we will help.

If you have concerns about Apple's iCloud Photo Library (which syncs your photos across devices), please review Apple's Privacy Policy at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

13. Summary

What Kipply Does:

  • ✅ Accesses your photo library with your permission
  • ✅ Processes photos locally on your device
  • ✅ Stores the Deleted queue and your statistics locally
  • ✅ Helps you delete unwanted photos
  • ✅ Shows ads, and measures how the app is used, as described in section 5

What Kipply Does NOT Do:

  • ❌ Upload your photos to the internet
  • ❌ Ask for your name, email or an account
  • ❌ Sell your data to anyone
  • ❌ Use your photos to target ads, or share them with anyone
  • ❌ Use your photos for any purpose other than displaying them to you

Your Privacy Matters.
We believe your photos are yours, and they stay on your device.
