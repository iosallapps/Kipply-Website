2. What Kipply does

Kipply helps you review and clean up the photos and videos in your iPhone or iPad Photos library: swipe to keep or delete, let Smart Clean group duplicates, similar shots, screenshots and videos, and browse older photos by date. It works only on photos and videos in your Photos library. It does not touch contacts, files, caches, app data or system storage.

6. Important: results and your responsibility

Suggestions, such as which photos look like duplicates or which shot is the best in a group, are made automatically and can be wrong. Storage figures are estimates. Review every suggestion before you delete anything.

Kipply never deletes a photo or video on its own. Items you swipe left wait in the Deleted tab, still in your Photos library, until you confirm. Then iOS asks for permission and moves them to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where iOS normally keeps them for 30 days; after that they cannot be recovered by Kipply or by us. If you use iCloud Photos, the deletion applies to your other devices through Apple. You are responsible for what you delete, so back up what you want to keep first.
