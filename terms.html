3. What Kipply Does

3.1 Photos and Videos Only

Kipply helps you review and clean up the photos and videos in your iPhone or iPad Photos library. You can swipe through them to keep or delete, and Kipply can suggest groups to review, such as duplicates, similar shots, screenshots and videos.

Kipply works only on the photos and videos in your Photos library. It does not clean caches, app data, other apps' files or system storage.

3.2 Access to Your Photos

Kipply needs access to your Photos library to work. You grant that access through iOS and can change it at any time in Settings. Your photos and videos are analysed on your device and are never uploaded by Kipply. Our Privacy Policy explains what data the services inside the App receive.

3.3 Suggestions and Estimates

Suggestions, such as which photos look like duplicates or which shot is the best in a group, are made automatically and can be wrong. Storage figures are estimates based on the size of the items you delete. Please review every suggestion before you delete anything.

4. Deleting Photos and Videos

4.1 You Decide What Is Deleted

Kipply never deletes a photo or video on its own. You choose what to delete, and you are responsible for those choices, including suggestions you accept.

4.2 How Deletion Works

  • Items you choose to delete first go to Kipply's bin, the Deleted tab. They stay in your Photos library until you confirm, and you can restore them from the bin.
  • When you confirm, iOS asks for your permission and moves the items to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app. Apple normally keeps them there for 30 days, and you can recover them from that album during that time.
  • After that, or if you empty Recently Deleted yourself, the items are permanently removed and cannot be recovered by Kipply or by us.
  • If you use iCloud Photos, deletions apply to all your devices that use the same library.

4.3 Back Up First

We strongly recommend that you back up any photos and videos you want to keep before using Kipply, for example with iCloud or another backup service.
