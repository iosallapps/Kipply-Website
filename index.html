How Kipply works

Five tabs. One lighter library.

Home, Swipe, Smart, Deleted and Discover: the same screens you will find in the app, each doing one job.

Swipe

One photo at a time.

Go through your library one photo at a time. Swipe left to delete, right to keep, or up to skip, and sort the pile newest, oldest or largest first.

  • Undo takes back your last swipeChanged your mind? One tap.
  • Newest, oldest or largest firstStart where the space is.

Smart Clean

Smart Clean finds the clutter.

Smart Clean scans your whole library on your device and gathers what you can clear. In each group of duplicates or similar shots, Kipply marks one to keep, and you can choose a different one with a tap.

  • Duplicates and similar photosExact copies, and shots that look alike.

Deleted tab

You decide what goes.

Swiped photos wait in the Deleted tab until you decide to restore them or delete them for good. Once you confirm, they move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where iOS keeps them for 30 days.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Kipply free?

Kipply is free to use, with 25 swipes a day, a limit on how many items you can delete at once, and ads. Kipply Pro is an auto-renewing subscription that removes both limits and the ads.

It is offered weekly or monthly at the price shown in the app for your region. If your Apple Account is eligible for a free trial, currently 3 days, its length is shown before you subscribe.

Do my photos leave my device?

No. Scanning, comparing and deleting all happen on your iPhone or iPad. Kipply has no account, and we have no access to your photos or videos.

What happens when I delete something?

Items you swipe left wait in the Deleted tab, still in your Photos library, until you confirm. When you confirm, iOS asks for your permission and moves them to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where Apple keeps them for 30 days.

After that they are gone for good, so back up anything you want to keep first.

Does Kipply clean caches, app data or system storage?

No. Kipply works only on the photos and videos in your Photos library.

Which devices does Kipply support?

Kipply requires iOS 17 or later and works on iPhone and iPad.

Does Kipply work offline?

Yes, for photos and videos stored on your device. Swiping, scanning and deleting work without an internet connection. You need a connection to load photos stored only in iCloud, to buy or restore Kipply Pro, and for ads to load.

How do I cancel Kipply Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Kipply. If you cancel, Kipply Pro stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
